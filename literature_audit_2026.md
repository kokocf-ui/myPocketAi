# Literature audit — 2026-11-24

## Current implementation in progress — release held

Literature is still work in progress. The user explicitly requested an interim APK on 2026-11-24; releasing that snapshot must not be described as completion across all countries. `node scripts/audit_literature_readiness.mjs --require-complete` intentionally fails: country-specific instructional and source review are still pending. The audit counts Romanian lesson-routing paths, not curricular completion, and it must never substitute automated counts for expert review.

Twelve Hungary primary year-focused chapters now have individual original lessons, Hungarian passages, explanations, five exercises and five distinct test questions in Romanian, English and Hungarian: three chapters in each of grades 1–4. They cover initial sound/letter distinctions, reading, event order, dialogue, summaries and writing. This is a partial increment, not four complete grades or a claim that all Hungarian spelling or the whole curriculum is covered. The Hungarian Education Authority's [NAT 2020 framework index](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/kerettantervek/2020_nat) links to the separate 1–4, 5–8 and secondary frameworks; independent teaching and source verification for the actual material is still required.

Three additional France CP chapters now have original lesson, worked example, exercises and test in French, Romanian and English, served from `public/education/literature/authored/FR/CP.json`. They address letters and syllables, comprehension and short-sentence writing, inspired by the [French cycle 2 programme](https://www.education.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/document/Annexe%203%20%E2%80%93%20Programme%20de%20fran%C3%A7ais%20du%20cycle%202-403818.pdf); [the 2024 order](https://www.education.gouv.fr/bo/2024/Hebdo41/MENE2415135A) applies from the 2025–26 school year, and [Éduscol](https://eduscol.education.gouv.fr/4347/enseigner-au-cycle-2) lists it for 2026–27. These are three partial teaching examples, not a complete CP course, an official test or an independently reviewed curriculum. Other CP chapters remain on earlier generic routes. The source-linked draft needs independent French-language and pedagogical review.

The embedded HTML approached its size budget. The embedded national catalogue now losslessly interns repeated strings, restoring headroom without changing the catalogue or offline behavior. Before adding large numbers of lessons, move authored year content to independently versioned, reader-integrated, cacheable packs and retain explicit offline-unavailable handling. This compact representation is not pack decoupling. Do not inflate the HTML or manufacture placeholder packs and call them a complete course.

## Scope

The Literature journey follows country → level → year → branch → chapter → activity. Framework links and advisory notices remain distinct from claims of a complete official curriculum. Regional requirements, prescribed books and university courses vary by jurisdiction, institution and year; this implementation does not certify them as exhaustive.

## What was verified and changed

- Older French metadata placed 6e in primary and 5e–3e in upper secondary, although the current selector places all four in lower secondary. Browser expansion now respects the selected stage and removes only stale generated rows from another stage; server expansion gives precedence to the current selector. Existing national rows are retained.
- The generated European practice chapters now have ten topic-matched original passages, worked explanations and six relevant questions each, in Romanian and English, with primary and secondary question sets. Exercise/test answers are not disclosed beforehand. They are teaching examples, not national examination papers or a full textbook.
- Existing non-Romanian national rows without a chapter-specific offline body first use their existing online route. If it fails, a limited RO/EN general subject-area reference is clearly labelled as **not** the full specific chapter, an official syllabus or a grammar rule for another language. It is never used to pretend to correct a learner's answers. Romanian and university authored paths remain intact.
- The workspace explains that offline examples may be in Romanian or English rather than the subject language, and that other languages or specific lessons may need internet. The reader itself carries the disclosure for exported or printed material.
- The new stage regression checks 624 school-year selections in 50 systems and the four RO/EN modes for 5,312 generated browser chapters. The activity regression checks passage/topic association, 1,380 pre-existing non-Romanian national rows with the explicitly limited fallback, all Romanian university units in all four activity modes, and actual serialized-client failure routing. These are coverage tests, not independent curricular or pedagogical certification.
- The real Chromium mobile test navigates Romania grade 9, France 6e and a poetry exercise, Hungary's honest offline fallback, and a university lesson with reader pagination and Back. Other country, age, language and device combinations are not all separately browser-tested.

## Pack and language decisions

Literature still embeds many lesson modules and materializes year rows lazily; it is **not** in `ALL_DECOUPLED_CURRICULUM_PACKS`. The `eduRunWithCurriculumPack` call itself still does not load Literature. Separate partial authored-year assets for HU grades 1–4 and FR CP are loaded through `literatureLoadAuthoredYear`, including cached offline use and retry. The old browser country-programme catalogue is also served through `public/education/literature/profiles/`; profiles are not year packs. The frontend Worker still includes programme data through server-side dependencies, and assets currently share the frontend deployment. The independent service is a local pilot, not activated in production. Full decoupling needs reader integration for all remaining content, compatible publication and deployment/offline tests. The generated examples are offline in RO/EN; selected Romanian university material is authored in RO. Other output languages or chapter-specific details can require the online service, and a general fallback is not equivalent to that missing detailed material.

Server and browser still construct some generated chapter wording differently. Stage identity is tested, but exact chapter-by-chapter equivalence and exhaustive national curricular coverage are not claimed. Further educational review and sourced, language-specific authoring remain appropriate for deeper national parity.
